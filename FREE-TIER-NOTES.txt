💰 NEED PERSISTENT DATA?

Upgrade to Render Starter Plan ($7/month):
  ✅ Persistent disk (1GB-10GB)
  ✅ Always-on (no cold starts)
  ✅ Better performance
  ✅ Database survives restarts

To add persistent disk after upgrading:
  1. Go to Render Dashboard
  2. Upgrade to Starter plan
  3. Add disk configuration:
     
     In render.yaml, add:
     disk:
       name: game-database
       mountPath: /opt/render/project/src/instance
       sizeGB: 1
